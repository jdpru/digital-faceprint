I own a small herd of cattle in the Volta region of Ghana. I grew up attending summer camps with names like "Farm and Wilderness" and "Farm Camp" which, looking back on it, was a brilliant way to get kids to do free labor. After connecting with a herder while I was abroad, I decided to go into business for myself.

I rented a car on Facebook Marketplace and purchased my first three cows at a cattle market. I continue to work with the aforementioned herder, who's become a friend, to raise these cows. I am oft asked if I balk at the ethics of owning and raising cattle for slaughter. So long as I continue to eat beef, not at all. Those two things are not morally distinct whatsoever, and if anything, if I'm going to eat beef I ought to understand what it takes to put it on my plate.

Against my better judgment, many of them have names. I give naming rights to friends, but somehow the naming convention converged. A few of the names include Moosilini, Moo Ze Dong, Benjamin Netanyamoo, and King Henry the Steak. I occasionally run funding rounds and give out equity stakes in my cows.
